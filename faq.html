FAQ

What is Foldly?

A website where you paint a flat square, watch it fold into a 3D origami model, and then share the result as a link, picture, or video.

Who is it for?

Kids, parents, and teachers. All four starting models are beginner-friendly.

Do I need real paper?

No, you can paint and fold on screen. But each model has step-by-step instructions if you want to fold it for real with one square of paper.

Which models can I fold?

Paper cup, cat face, dog face, and tulip. MarCO is on the Paper models shelf: a net of a CubeSat, not a fold from one square.

How do I share my fold?

Use Copy link, Download picture (PNG), or Download video on the share panel. A link holds about 25 strokes. Past that, download a picture or a video.

Can I use it in class?

Yes. Every model page has a teacher note with classroom tips.
