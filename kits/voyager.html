Voyager paper kit

Print these four nets at 100% scale. Each one fits on A4 and on US Letter with margins. Cut on the solid lines, fold on the dashed lines, and glue only the shaded tabs.

The body is a ten-sided box. The dish is eight triangles that close into a shallow cone. The science boom and the power boom glue to the body at their mouth tabs. This kit leaves out the long magnetometer boom and the wire antennas.

Some sizes are stylised so the parts fit on paper: the dish depth, the boom widths, the scan platform, the strut length, and the 20° tilt of the power boom. The ten sides of the body, the 3.66 m dish, and the boom lengths follow the published spacecraft.

Inspired by NASA/JPL-Caltech's Voyager. Not endorsed by NASA.
