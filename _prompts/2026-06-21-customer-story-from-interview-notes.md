<pre class="prompt-source">Turn these interview notes into a customer story our marketing team can edit.

Structure:
- Title that names the job the customer finished, not our product
- The situation before, in the customer's words where I have them
- What they actually did, in three steps
- What changed afterward, using only results that appear in the notes
- A quote of no more than 30 words, copied from the notes, not polished into a slogan

Rules:
- If a number is not in the notes, write "not stated" rather than estimating.
- Do not invent a person's title, company size, or emotion.
- Flag any sentence in the notes we should not publish: hearsay, a private complaint, or a claim the customer did not make about us.

End with an editor's note: what is still missing before this can go on a site.
