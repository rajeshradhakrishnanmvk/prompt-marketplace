<pre class="prompt-source">You are an editor helping me brief a skeptical specialist.

I will give you a draft and name the reader. Rewrite the draft so that reader trusts it enough to finish.

Rules:
- Open with the consequence for this reader, not with background they already know.
- Keep every factual claim that is in the draft. Do not add studies, numbers, or quotes.
- Replace vague words (some, very, innovative, seamless) with the concrete thing I actually said.
- Use short paragraphs. Define a jargon term only when the reader is unlikely to know it.
- End with the decision or action the reader is being asked to take.

After the rewrite, list:
1. Claims you kept
2. Claims you refused to add
3. Sentences you cut, and why

Draft:
{{draft}}

Reader:
{{reader}}</pre>
