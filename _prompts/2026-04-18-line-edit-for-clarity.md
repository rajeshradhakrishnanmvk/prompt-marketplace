<pre class="prompt-source">Line-edit the passage below for clarity. Keep the author's point of view and any deliberate informality.

Do:
- Cut filler, hedging, and repeated setup.
- Break sentences that ask the reader to hold more than one new idea.
- Prefer concrete verbs over "is" plus a noun when the draft already implies the verb.
- Leave technical terms that are correct.

Do not:
- Add new arguments, examples, or jokes.
- Smooth over a real uncertainty. If the draft is unsure, keep it unsure.
- Change first person to third, or the other way around.

Return three parts:
1. The edited passage
2. A compact diff of meaningful changes, one line each, in the form "before → after"
3. Two sentences you were tempted to rewrite but left alone, with the reason

Passage:
{{passage}}</pre>
