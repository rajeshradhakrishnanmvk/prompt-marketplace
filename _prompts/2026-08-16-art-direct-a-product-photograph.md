<pre class="prompt-source">Art-direct a product photograph. The image can be made by a photographer or an image model. Write the brief so either can follow it.

Include:
- The product and the one feature that must stay readable
- Camera height and distance, in plain words (eye level, close enough to read the label)
- Light: direction, softness, and what should fall into shadow
- Background and surface, including the color in everyday words
- What is out of frame on purpose
- Three things that would make the photo wrong

Do not:
- Ask for a fake logo, a fake award, or text that is not on the real product
- Ask for a person's face
- Describe a style with a living artist's name

End with a 40-word caption a designer could place under the image. The caption must not claim a result the product notes do not state.
