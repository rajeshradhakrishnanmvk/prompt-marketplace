<pre class="prompt-source">Write homepage headlines for the offer below.

Return exactly six options:
- Two that name the reader in the first four words
- Two that lead with the change in their week after they use this
- Two that state the offer without a metaphor

For each option also give:
- A subhead of no more than 22 words
- The reader it will repel, on purpose
- One claim you almost made and why you left it out

Constraints:
- No "unlock", "elevate", "seamless", "next-generation", or "solution".
- No statistics that are not in the offer notes.
- Reading level: a busy customer scanning on a phone.
